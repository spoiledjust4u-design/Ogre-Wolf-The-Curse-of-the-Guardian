Level Progression

The four teenagers must travel through dangerous locations surrounding Ravenwood to find the six missing children. Each level reveals more about Kingsley, the mysterious Ogre-Wolf, and the curse that transformed the Guardian.

Level Location Difficulty Objective
1 Ravenwood Village Easy Gather clues about the missing children.
2 The Dark Forest Easy Follow the children's trail and uncover the truth about the Ogre-Wolf.
3 Kingsley's Den Medium Discover Kingsley's secret and find the Guardian's key.
4 Hollow Kingdom Medium Discover the ancient kingdom beneath Ravenwood and uncover the Hollow King's secrets.
5 The Final Guardian Hard Find the Guardian Sword, defeat the darkness, and prove you are worthy Guardians.
6 The Final Darkness Hard Destroy the ancient darkness beneath Ravenwood and complete the final mission.

Warning

Not everything in the forest is what it seems. The Ogre-Wolf may appear to be the enemy, but the deeper you travel into Ravenwood, the more you will question who the real villain is.

Your choices matter. The decisions made during the journey can lead to different endings.
