Meet the Characters

Four teenagers enter the dark forest surrounding Ravenwood after six children mysteriously disappear. The villagers blame a terrifying creature known as the Ogre-Wolf.

But the teenagers soon discover that the creature has a name: Kingsley.

The deeper they travel into the forest, the more they realize that Kingsley may not be the monster everyone believes him to be.

Kingsley: The Ogre-Wolf

Kingsley is a powerful creature with the strength of an ogre and the instincts of a wolf. His frightening appearance has caused the villagers to fear him for generations.

Long ago, Kingsley was the Guardian of the forest. He protected Ravenwood from creatures that came from beneath the earth. A corrupted ritual changed him into the Ogre-Wolf.

Although Kingsley looks dangerous, he continues to protect the forest and the innocent people who enter it.

Kingsley's Secret

Kingsley's greatest weakness is not a weapon. It is the curse controlling him.

The teenagers must discover the truth about the curse before they can decide whether Kingsley should be defeated or saved.

The Strategy: Learn Kingsley's history, discover the Guardian symbols, and uncover the person responsible for the curse.

The Mystery of the Missing Children

The teenagers believe the six children were taken by Kingsley. However, evidence found throughout the forest suggests something else.

The children may have been following someone into the forest before they disappeared.

Who led them there?

And why does Kingsley seem determined to keep the teenagers away from the deepest part of the forest?
