The Mission Begins

Six children have disappeared into the deep, dark forest surrounding the village of Ravenwood. The villagers blame a terrifying creature known as the Ogre-Wolf.

You have been chosen to enter the forest, find the missing children, and uncover the truth behind the mysterious beast.

But the deeper you travel into the forest, the more you begin to realize that the Ogre-Wolf may not be your enemy.

Something much more dangerous is hiding in the darkness.

Begin the Journey

The Legend of the Ogre-Wolf

Long ago, a warrior named Kingsley protected Ravenwood from creatures that lived beneath the forest. Kingsley became known as the Guardian.

But when the villagers became afraid of his power, they attempted to control the Guardian's magic.

Something went terribly wrong.

Kingsley was transformed into a massive creature with the strength of an ogre and the instincts of a wolf.

The villagers called him the Ogre-Wolf.

They believed he had become a monster.

The truth may be very different.
